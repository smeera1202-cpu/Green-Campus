Campus Gardening & Organic Care Handbook

🌳 1. Campus Tree & Shrub Watering Guidelines

Drip irrigation should be activated early morning (6:00 - 8:00 AM) to minimize evaporation. Maintain soil moisture levels between 40% and 65% for optimal root aeration.

✂️ 2. Pruning & Canopy Management

Prune dead or diseased branches quarterly. Cut at a 45-degree angle 1/4 inch above healthy buds to encourage outward foliage growth.

🌿 3. Organic Pest Control (Neem Spray Recipe)

Mix 5 ml of cold-pressed organic Neem oil and 2 ml of eco liquid soap in 1 Liter of warm water. Spray thoroughly on infected leaves to eliminate aphids and spider mites.
